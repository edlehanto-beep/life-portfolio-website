Life Portfolio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Life Portfolio is designed to help you reflect on different areas of your life and consider how life events may affect your personal wellbeing and priorities.

Your privacy is central to how the app works. Life Portfolio is designed to keep your portfolio information on your device, without requiring an account or automatically uploading your personal data.

1. Information stored in the app

Life Portfolio allows you to create and manage personal information, including:

  • Life Area scores and baseline values.
  • Events, anticipated impacts, and personal notes.
  • Reviews, reflections, and changes to your portfolio over time.
  • App preferences and backup reminder settings.

This information may be sensitive. It is stored locally on your device.

Life Portfolio does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other identifying information to use the app.

2. Data collection and sharing

Life Portfolio does not transmit your portfolio information to the developer.

The app does not use advertising, third-party analytics, tracking technologies, or an external account system.

The developer does not have access to your portfolio content through the app.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Local storage and security

Your portfolio is stored locally using Apple's on-device data storage technologies.

Life Portfolio uses the security protections provided by iOS and iPadOS. However, no method of digital storage can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate security on your device and protecting any backup files you create.

4. Encrypted backups

Life Portfolio offers optional manual backup and restore functionality.

When you create a backup:

  • Your portfolio information is encrypted using AES-256-GCM.
  • Encryption is protected by a password you choose.
  • You decide where to save the encrypted backup file using Apple's Files interface.
  • Backups are not automatically uploaded by Life Portfolio.

You may choose local storage, iCloud Drive, or another available storage provider.

If you choose a cloud storage provider, your backup file will also be subject to that provider's privacy and security practices.

Life Portfolio does not retain your backup password and cannot recover a forgotten password.

The app may remind you to create a backup. These reminders operate locally and do not require push notifications.

5. Apple services and TestFlight

When Life Portfolio is distributed through Apple's App Store or TestFlight, Apple may process information related to app installation, usage, diagnostics, crashes, and testing in accordance with Apple's own privacy practices.

During TestFlight testing, the developer may receive tester information, crash reports, and feedback made available through Apple's testing services.

This information is used to evaluate, troubleshoot, and improve Life Portfolio. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

TestFlight feedback may include comments or screenshots you choose to submit. Please avoid including sensitive personal information unless necessary.

Apple's privacy practices are described at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your portfolio information remains on your device until you edit or delete it, restore a different portfolio, or remove the app and its locally stored data.

Because your portfolio data is stored locally, the developer cannot remotely access or delete it for you.

Encrypted backup files remain wherever you choose to save them until you delete them.

Uninstalling Life Portfolio does not automatically delete backup files stored separately in Files, iCloud Drive, or another storage provider.

7. Your privacy choices

You control the information you enter into Life Portfolio.

You can edit or delete Events, update your portfolio, and choose whether to create an encrypted backup.

You can turn off optional backup reminders.

You do not need to enable cloud storage to use Life Portfolio.

If you contact the developer or submit TestFlight feedback, you may request information about, or deletion of, personal information held by the developer in connection with that communication, subject to applicable law.

8. Children's privacy

Life Portfolio is not specifically designed for children. The app does not intentionally collect personal information from children through its portfolio functionality.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's functionality, data handling practices, or legal obligations change.

The current version will be made available through the app's published privacy policy link.
